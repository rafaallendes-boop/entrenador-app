# Design System: RallyIQ
**Project ID:** 11513637511751447524

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
RallyIQ is a premium, high-performance intelligent coaching application. The atmosphere is "Deportiva-Tech": minimalist, elegant, serious, and deeply technological. It rejects the generic fitness look to embrace a startup-premium aesthetic. Born from elite squash training, it feels like a modern AI tool combined with high-performance sports science. The environment is inherently Dark Mode to minimize distraction and emphasize data.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Deep Obsidian Base** (#0D1117) - Dark background to enhance contrast and convey a premium tech feel.
* **Electric Kinetic Green** (#00FF66) - Primary action color for CTAs, highlighting progress, and active states. Vibrates against the dark base.
* **Hyper Blue Accent** (#007BFF) - Secondary accent for data visualization and tech-focused elements.
* **Crisp Slate Gray** (#8B949E) - For secondary text and structural boundaries without being heavy.
* **Pure White** (#FFFFFF) - For primary readability and critical headlines.

## 3. Typography Rules
Clean, modern sans-serif typography.
* **Headings:** Inter or Lexend. Crisp, precise, and tech-forward. High contrast.
* **Body:** Inter. Highly legible for dense data and clear communication.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons:** Sharp or subtly rounded (4px to 8px), flat colors with intense electric hover states. No heavy gradients.
*   **Cards/Containers:** Clean boundaries with subtle `surface_container`, maybe 1px slate ghost borders. Deep spatial layering instead of harsh dropshadows.
*   **Inputs/Forms:** Minimalist, active states lit up in Electric Green.

## 5. Layout Principles
Ample whitespace, crisp alignments, bento-box style grids for disciplines and features. Clear visual hierarchy emphasizing conversion (login/signup) and value propositions.
